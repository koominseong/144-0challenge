# KBO Player Life v7 changes

## Military
- Military deferment is allowed through age 29.
- At age 30, a player who is still unserved is forced to make a military choice before the season can proceed.
- Active-duty service and Sangmu service both take exactly 2 seasons.
- Sangmu application has an acceptance check; on acceptance the team changes to Sangmu Phoenix for the service period and returns to the original club after completion.
- Military seasons produce no official KBO games/statistics.

## Dynamic yearly choices
- Training: 100 prebuilt candidates.
- Life/family: 100 prebuilt candidates, with relationship-stage choices integrated.
- Agent: 100 candidates.
- GM: 100 candidates.
- Manager: 100 candidates.
- The UI shows 4 context-sensitive choices per category/year instead of the same fixed buttons every year.
- Choices consider age, position, stamina, fame, family, relationship, contract timing, veteran status and overseas/national-team context.
- Choices include positive, neutral, trade-off and negative outcomes.
